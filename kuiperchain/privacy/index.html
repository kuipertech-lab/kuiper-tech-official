kuiperChain Privacy Policy

Owner: Kuiper Technologies Pty Ltd. Last updated: 11 July 2026.

kuiperChain is enterprise ledger infrastructure. Public website inquiries collect only the information a prospective customer chooses to provide by email or during licensing discussions.

Data Controller

Kuiper Technologies Pty Ltd is responsible for kuiperChain product, licensing, and related support communications.

Data We May Collect

  • Business contact details provided in licensing, support, or partnership inquiries.
  • Company, role, project, and procurement information voluntarily shared during commercial discussions.
  • Technical environment details required to assess integration, deployment, security, or licensing fit.
  • Operational records required to manage demonstrations, proposals, contracts, support, and security review processes.

Data We Do Not Intend to Collect Through the Website

The public website is not intended to collect production secrets, private keys, wallet recovery material, confidential source code, regulated personal records, payment card credentials, or live corporate automation data.

How We Use Data

  • To respond to product, licensing, support, and partnership inquiries.
  • To assess commercial fit, prepare proposals, and manage licensing discussions.
  • To support security, audit, deployment, and compliance review processes.
  • To maintain business records where required for legal, tax, security, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Security

Kuiper Technologies uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards for business communications and product records. Prospective customers should not send sensitive credentials, private keys, or production secrets by ordinary email.

Retention

Business inquiry and licensing records are retained only for as long as needed to manage the commercial relationship, comply with legal obligations, support security review, resolve disputes, or maintain operational records.

Changes to This Policy

Kuiper Technologies may update this policy as kuiperChain evolves or as legal, security, or commercial requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
