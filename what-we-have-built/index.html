coNNect

coNNect is a hyper-localized, privacy-first social platform built to instantly bridge the gap between digital proximity and real-world community for international travelers, backpackers, and expats.

Backed by the secure infrastructure of Kuiper Technologies, the app uses a 350-meter fuzzed mapping system, country-of-origin community matching, secure messaging, trusted contacts, safety controls, moderation workflows, account deletion support, encrypted local storage, and review-ready privacy documentation.

kuiperChain

kuiperChain is an institutional-grade, zero-trust ledger infrastructure engineered to serve as the security foundation for corporate operating systems, proprietary platforms, audit records, automation ecosystems, and protected data transitions.

The system combines layered ledger design, device-anchored authorization, isolated test environments, security documentation, and commercial licensing materials for organizations that need tamper-resistant execution history.

Rig Genesis

Developed by Kuiper Technologies, Rig Genesis is a planetary-scale waste-deletion network that transforms decommissioned offshore oil platforms into autonomous ocean plastic processing hubs.

By tracking every kilogram of neutralized polymer through an unhackable blockchain ledger and a purpose-built SaaS platform that calculates, monitors, and verifies corporate Plastic Offset Credit purchases, Rig Genesis turns a multi-billion dollar industrial liability into premium, auditable environmental assets.
