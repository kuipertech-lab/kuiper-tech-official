Kuiper OS

Kuiper Technologies is pioneering a world defining proprietary unified, deterministic Operating System for Autonomous Manufacturing and Industrial Automation, turning fragmented factory floors into programmable, self-optimizing Physical APIs.

Built on a zero-garbage-collection C++20 architecture running on Linux RT-PREEMPT kernels, our platform unifies raw physics, industrial fieldbuses, microsecond motion execution, multi-protocol translation, and cloud-native AI orchestration across five tightly integrated layers and multiple Sub layers.

Once all five main layers along with multiple sub layers are deployed, Kuiper OS transforms legacy and modern industrial machinery-from multi-axis CNCs and robotic arms to PLCs and sensors-into a vendor-agnostic, software-defined ecosystems.

Backed by OpenSSL 3.0 cryptographic verification and POSIX O_SYNC journaling for hardware-to-cloud auditability, Kuiper OS enables engineers, developers, and AI systems to program, query, and automate complex physical machinery as effortlessly as calling a modern web service.

Kuiper Battery

Kuiper Technologies is pioneering The Kuiper Battery Module a containerized, hybrid energy storage platform engineered to bridge the critical gap between instantaneous high-megawatt pulse power absorption and long-duration bulk energy firming.

Unlike conventional lithium-ion systems that suffer from rapid chemical degradation, thermal runaway risks, and slow response times when exposed to heavy surge loads, the Kuiper Battery utilizes a multi-tier, non-combustible architecture capable of absorbing extreme current spikes in micro-fractions of a second while delivering active grid harmonic filtering and sub-millisecond blackout protection..

Designed to operate as the exclusive power-conditioning shock absorber for the rAsphere fusion platform, the module captures intense plasma compression surges and safely smooths them into clean, grid-compliant energy without exposing municipal utility infrastructure to electrical stress.

The Kuiper Battery can be licensed to be used in any operation that uses lithium based batteries, from solar panel energy capture to hotels using solar or green energies and want to keep them on site and not re grid with out the dangers of thermal runaway.

rAsphere

At Kuiper Technologies, we are shifting the energy paradigm from resource extraction to subatomic engineering. The rAsphere is a 750 mm3 modular inertial fusion reactor configured to run safely on a non-radioactive fuel cycle.

The long-range goal is a compact reactor platform capable of turning buildings, remote facilities, and critical infrastructure into sovereign energy islands with no grid dependency, no lithium battery reliance, and no operational emissions at the point of use.

The rAsphere concept is built around standardized power blades, high-powered magnetic compression, direct electricity capture, and a software-defined control layer designed to coordinate reactor telemetry, thermal behavior, safety states, and clean-water infrastructure from one deterministic console.

Our software control console is built, and the platform is in R&D stage as is being shaped for simulation, engineering validation, and future manufacturing partnerships as Kuiper Technologies works toward anchoring the macro-hydrogen transition.

Green Steel OS

Green Steel OS is a monolithic, Rust-based industrial operating system engineered to automate zero-emission, hydrogen-based steel plants by replacing slow, fragmented legacy SCADA systems with ultra-fast, sub-cycle execution.

Designed specifically for Direct Reduced Iron plants and multi-megawatt Electric Arc Furnaces, Green Steel OS dynamically intercepts power streams from wind parks, utility grids, and battery storage to deliver sub-8-millisecond automated load-shedding during energy drops.

The platform automatically synchronizes pure green hydrogen flow rates with real-time electric arc furnace fluctuations to maintain optimal thermodynamics with pure water-vapor emissions, while continuously scanning factory-wide sensors to execute instant, native hardware safety isolation without human reaction lag.

Green Steel OS unifies power management, precise chemical balancing, and active plant safety into a single, rock-solid platform for the next generation of clean industrial steel production.
