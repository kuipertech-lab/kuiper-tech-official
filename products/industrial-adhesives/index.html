Materials

Recyclable Industrial Adhesives

Two 100% completely recyclable, high-strength glue systems are in development to help remove toxic, petroleum-based hardeners from manufacturing workflows.

Product Direction

Kuiper Technologies is developing adhesive alternatives for industries that need strong bonding performance without locking products into petroleum-heavy, difficult-to-recycle material systems.

Target Use Cases

  • Manufacturing workflows that currently rely on petroleum-derived hardeners.
  • Packaging, assembly, and industrial material systems that need cleaner end-of-life pathways.
  • Partner trials where recyclability, strength, and process compatibility can be measured under written evaluation terms.

Commercial Status

This product line is part of the Kuiper Technologies R&D and prototype pipeline. Technical specifications, samples, performance data, and partner access are available only through written commercial or evaluation arrangements.
